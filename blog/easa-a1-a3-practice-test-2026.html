EASA A1/A3 Practice Test

Online practice tests are useful because the A1/A3 assessment covers more than one area of drone knowledge. A good study routine combines the official rules with repeated multiple-choice practice, especially in topics where you make mistakes.

EASA explains that an A1/A3 remote pilot certificate is obtained through the relevant online training and theoretical examination requirements for the Open Category. The exact administrative process can depend on the competent authority in the country where you are taking the examination.

Official reference: EASA Easy Access Rules for Unmanned Aircraft Systems.

What is the EASA A1/A3 drone exam?

The A1/A3 qualification is part of the European framework for drone operations in the Open Category. Depending on the drone and the operation, a remote pilot may need to complete online training and pass an online theoretical examination before flying.

EASA lists A1/A3 competence requirements for several common drone operations, including operations involving C1 drones and many A3 operations. Always check the current rules for your drone class, operation and country before flying.

What topics should you study for A1/A3?

Your preparation should cover the rules and knowledge needed to operate safely. Important areas include aviation regulations, operational procedures, UAS general knowledge, meteorology, flight performance, airspace and safety considerations, and privacy and data protection.

The practice questions on this page intentionally cover different subjects so you can quickly identify the areas where you need more revision.

How can I prepare for the A1/A3 drone licence?

Start with the official rules, then practise multiple-choice questions until you can explain why an answer is correct. Do not rely only on memorising the answer position. Understanding the underlying rule makes it much easier to handle differently worded questions on an exam.

A useful routine is to study one topic at a time, complete a practice set, review every mistake, and then repeat the questions later. This is particularly helpful for regulations, operational procedures and meteorology, where small wording changes can alter the correct answer.

A1/A3 Drone Licence FAQ

What is the EASA A1/A3 drone certificate?

The A1/A3 remote pilot certificate is used for relevant Open Category drone operations. Depending on the drone and operation, remote pilots may need to complete online training and pass an online theoretical examination.

How do I prepare for the A1/A3 drone exam?

Study the relevant A1/A3 rules and practise multiple-choice questions across the main subject areas. Focus on understanding why each answer is correct and revisit the topics where you make mistakes.
