5 Sample Questions From the Real Exam Style

These are the types that make people fail. Can you get them right?

1. What is the maximum altitude allowed in Open Category A1/A3?
  • A) 100m AGL
  • B) 120m AGL ✓
  • C) 150m AGL
  • D) No limit if VLOS
Explanation: EU Regulation 2019/947 sets 120m AGL as absolute max in Open Category, even with C0-C4 drones. This is the #1 question everyone gets wrong.
2. You fly a DJI Mini 4 Pro (249g with camera). Do you need A1/A3 certificate?
  • A) No, sub-250g needs nothing
  • B) Yes, because it has a camera and needs operator registration ✓
  • C) Only if you fly over people
  • D) Only A2 is required
Explanation: Even sub-250g drones with a camera require operator registration and A1/A3 in most EASA states. This traps many Mini owners.
3. What does VLOS mean?
  • A) Video Line of Sight via screen
  • B) Visual Line of Sight - direct eye contact without aids (except glasses) ✓
  • C) Virtual Line of Sight with binoculars
  • D) Verified Location System
Explanation: VLOS = you must see your drone with your own eyes at all times. FPV screen alone is not VLOS - you need an observer.
4. A drone is labeled C1. In which subcategory can you fly it?
  • A) A1 (can fly over uninvolved people, but not over crowds) ✓
  • B) Only A3
  • C) Only A2
  • D) Specific Category only
Explanation: C0 and C1 = A1. C2 = A2 (30m from people) or A3. C3/C4 = A3 only (150m from residential/commercial).
5. Can you fly at night in Open Category?
  • A) Never
  • B) Yes, if your drone has a green flashing light ✓
  • C) Only with A2 certificate
  • D) Only in A3
Explanation: Night flying is allowed since 2024 if you have a flashing green light for visibility. Many old guides still say no.
