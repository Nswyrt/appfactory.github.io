Why do people fail these?

1. 120m trap: The 120m limit follows terrain under drone, not take-off point. Taking off from a hill and flying over a valley puts you 200m above ground = illegal. This is in 80% of exams.

2. Manned aircraft: Many think helicopter will see them. No. You must descend and land immediately. Manned always has right of way.

3. LiPo battery: Swollen battery = internal gas = fire risk. Never fly, charge or store it. Dispose safely.

4. GDPR: Even hobby pilots have data protection duties when capturing identifiable people. Saving video counts as storing personal data.

5. Thunderstorm gust front: Dangerous wind can hit you kilometers ahead of visible storm. Clear sky above you doesn't mean safe.
